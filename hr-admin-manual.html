SparkX HR Administrator Manual

Complete visual reference for HR Administrators in SparkX — covering sign-in, the HR Admin Dashboard, all six HR sidebar modules, and all six Management governance modules.

Module 01: Sign-In & Authentication

Authenticate with your HR Admin credentials (for staging walkthrough: vanessa.wu@nautilus.test) to unlock HR and Management workspace menus.

01
Authentication /login

HR Admin Sign-In Page

Enter your Email and Password (with optional Show password toggle) and click Sign in. Alternative sign-in options include Google OAuth and Sign in with Biometrics.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 01 of 42
  • 1Navigate to the SparkX portal URL (/login) in your desktop browser.
  • 2Enter your HR Admin Email (e.g. vanessa.wu@nautilus.test) and Password; use the eye icon to verify your password if needed.
  • 3Click Sign in (or use Google / Sign in with Biometrics) to open your HR Admin workspace.

Module 02: Dashboard & Sidebar Navigation

Monitor pending inbox items, document requests, and team leave schedules from the Home Dashboard while navigating HR and Management modules from the left sidebar.

02
Dashboard /ess

Home Dashboard & Summary Metrics

Displays a personalized greeting, active announcement banner, and customizable KPI cards: Pending Inbox, Leave Balance, Pending Claims, and + Add Metric.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 02 of 42
  • 1Review the top Announcement Banner and click Learn More → to open active company-wide notices.
  • 2Check your Pending Inbox, Leave Balance, and Pending Claims KPI cards for immediate action items.
  • 3Click + Add Metric on the right to customize additional dashboard KPIs.
03
Sidebar Menu Navigation

Sidebar HR & Management Menus

Beneath the Organization Switcher and Employee Self-Service links, HR Admins see the HR section (6 modules) and Management section (6 modules), plus their HR Admin user badge and floating AI Assistant.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 03 of 42
  • 1Use the Organization switcher at the top-left to confirm or change your active company group (e.g. PT. Nautilus Group).
  • 2Scroll the left sidebar down to the HR section to access Profile Requests, Pending Approvals, Organization Chart, Onboarding Admin, Offboarding Admin, and Distribute Documents.
  • 3Scroll further to the Management section for Announcements, Finance Operations, Reports, Configuration, Master Data, and Audit Trail.
04
Widgets /ess

Priority Tasks & Team on Leave

The lower dashboard surfaces Priority Tasks (e.g., pending Employee Reference Letter requests with Review buttons), Activity Feed, and Team on Leave (This Week).

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 04 of 42
  • 1Scroll down the Dashboard to inspect Priority Tasks (such as pending Document: Employee Reference Letter requests).
  • 2Click Review on any task card to open and process it, or click View All > to see the full queue.
  • 3Check Activity Feed and Team on Leave (This Week) on the right to monitor upcoming absences.

Module 03: HR → Profile Requests

Review and audit employee profile update requests assigned to you as HRBP / HR Admin.

05
HR Menu /ess/management/profile-requests

Profile Change Requests Queue

Top summary cards track Pending Review vs. Resolved requests. Each card lists employee name, NIK, request ID (PRF-00079), modified section count, date, and status pill.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 05 of 42
  • 1Open HR → Profile Requests from the left sidebar.
  • 2Check the Pending Review and Resolved summary cards at the top to gauge your workload.
  • 3Scan each request card for employee name, NIK, request ID (e.g. PRF-00079), modified section count, and status pill.
06
Detail View Approved

Expanded Approved Request

Clicking an approved request expands a green confirmation banner recording the exact timestamp when the profile changes were applied.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 06 of 42
  • 1Click an approved request card (such as PRF-00079) to expand its audit details.
  • 2Verify the green confirmation banner showing the exact timestamp when the profile changes were applied.
07
Detail View Rejected

Expanded Rejected Request

Clicking a rejected request displays the recorded Rejection reason note for auditability. Approve/Reject buttons appear only on pending items.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 07 of 42
  • 1Click a rejected request card (such as PRF-00074) to expand its details.
  • 2Review the recorded Rejection reason note. For Pending Review items, use the Approve/Reject buttons that appear inside the expanded card.

Module 04: HR → Pending Approvals

Triage every request still waiting on an approver, copy decision links to follow up, or co-assign an alternate approver.

08
HR Menu /ess/management/pending-approvals

Approvals Triage & Filters

Three summary cards track Waiting, Overdue (>3 days), and Approver can't act (deactivated login). Filter by search query, module, company, or Overdue only.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 08 of 42
  • 1Open HR → Pending Approvals to view every request currently waiting on an approver.
  • 2Monitor the Waiting, Overdue (>3 days), and Approver can't act summary cards.
  • 3Use the search box, Module dropdown, Company dropdown, and Overdue only toggle to narrow down stalled items.
09
Step Trail Copy Link

Expanded Approval Steps

Expand any card to inspect the step-by-step approval chain. Use Copy link to copy the assigned approver's direct decision link and send a reminder.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 09 of 42
  • 1Click the expand chevron on a request card (e.g. LR-MU1Z3PLI) to inspect its step-by-step approval chain and current assignee.
  • 2Click Copy link to copy the assigned approver's direct decision link and send them a reminder.
10
Delegation Add Approver

Add Alternate Approver Modal

Click Add approver to co-assign an active-login user with company access. Enter a Reason and click Add & get link — whoever decides first resolves the step.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 10 of 42
  • 1If the current approver is unavailable or cannot act, click Add approver on the request card.
  • 2Search by name or employee number under Person to add (active-login users with company access, excluding the requester) and enter a Reason.
  • 3Click Add & get link to co-assign the alternate approver (whichever approver decides first resolves the step).
