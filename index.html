Alternate Scenario • Tenant Verification Negative Case • Rejection & Resubmission

What If Your Organization Request is Rejected?

If legal entity information cannot be verified, SparkX superadmins will decline the request and provide specific feedback for resubmission.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Rejection Guide
  • 1Why Rejection Occurs: Requests are rejected if the legal entity name does not match official tax records, the registration number (NPWP) is invalid, or details are insufficient to verify the organization.
  • 2Email Rejection Notice: You will receive an email titled “About your request for [Company Name]” with the explanation: “We reviewed your request to set up [Company Name] on SparkX and couldn't approve it. The details weren't enough for us to verify the organization.”
  • 3Web Portal Rejection Banner: Logging in to the SparkX web app displays an alert banner explaining the decision and inviting a new request.
  • 4How to Resubmit: Click Open SparkX (or log in), review the feedback, click Create a new organization, and re-submit your registration with complete, accurate legal documents and verified corporate credentials.
Need assistance with verification? Contact the SparkX support team with your corporate registration documents for priority verification.

Employee Onboarding • Access Requirements

Welcome to SparkX Employee Self-Service (ESS)

Your official step-by-step guideline to activating your personal employee account, setting security credentials, and exploring the ESS platform.

Modern Digital Workplace for Employees

SparkX Employee Self-Service (ESS) empowers you to perform daily attendance clock-in, request leaves, track reimbursement claims, and manage your employment profile with complete transparency and zero paperwork.

Strict Access Policy • Invitation Only

Important: Access is Strictly by HR Invitation Only

Unlike HR Administrators or Company Founders who can register a new organization, regular employees cannot register directly on the SparkX website. If you attempt to sign up directly via the public registration screen, the system will assume you are creating a new company and will prompt you for corporate legal details, permanent operating currency, and corporate tax documents (NPWP). Regular employees cannot join an existing employer’s workspace through public sign-up.

To access your company’s Employee Self-Service (ESS) workspace, your company HR Administrator must first register your profile in the corporate employee directory and dispatch an official email invitation. Each invitation contains a secure, cryptographically signed one-time activation link that is strictly valid for 7 calendar days and bound directly to your designated email address.

✕
No Public Sign-Up Direct registration is only for setting up new legal entities.
✉
Issued by HR Admin Your corporate email must be registered in the company directory.
⏱
7-Day Expiration Tokens expire after 7 days and must be re-sent if unused.
Haven't received an invitation email yet? Check with your company HR Admin or manager to ensure your work email address was added to the employee directory.

Browser or Smartphone

2.1 • Attendance

2.1 Clock-in / Clock-out (WFO & WFH)

Record your daily shift start and end times directly from the Time & Attendance module with automatic browser geolocation.

2.1a
Shift Start WFO & WFH

Open Time & Attendance and Clock In

SparkX automatically detects your location and classifies your work type (WFO or WFH) based on your company's configured office geofence sites.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Clock-In Flow
  • 1Click Time & Attendance in the left ESS sidebar (or mobile menu) to open your daily shift tracker.
  • 2Allow browser location access when prompted so the Location indicator resolves your current area instead of staying on Detecting location....
  • 3Click the large green CLOCK IN button. Your check-in timestamp is recorded immediately without an extra confirmation modal.
  • 4Your status changes from Checked Out to Working, the button turns red (CLOCK OUT), and the CHECK IN counter updates with your exact time.
Automatic WFO vs. WFH Detection: There is no manual WFO/WFH toggle. SparkX compares your GPS coordinates against your company's office geofence sites—clocking in inside an office radius logs WFO, while clocking in outside logs WFH.
2.1b
Shift End & Logs Timesheets

Monitor Working Hours, View Details & Clock Out

Track live status in My Timesheets, inspect geolocation map links, and clock out at the end of your workday.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Clock-Out & Review
  • 1While clocked in, your row appears in My Timesheets with status POSTED (and a red LATE badge if after shift start).
  • 2Click any row in My Timesheets to open Timesheet Details, showing Check-In, Clock Out, View Location (opens Google Maps), Final Status, and Work Type (WFH or WFO).
  • 3At the end of your shift, click the red CLOCK OUT button. Status updates to Finished (Clocked Out) and the button disables for the rest of the day.
One Shift Cycle Per Day: Once you click CLOCK OUT, the button becomes disabled (gray) for the current date. Double-check that your workday is complete before clocking out.
2.2 • Attendance Corrections

2.2 Attendance Corrections & Missed Punches

How to verify recorded timestamps and what to do if you miss a clock-in, miss a clock-out, or have a geolocation issue.

2.2a
Audit & Escalation HR Admin Assisted

Verify Timesheet Details & Escalate Corrections

Inspect your recorded punch times in My Timesheets before contacting HR for manual adjustments.

How to Handle Corrections

Verification
  • 1Open Time & Attendance and locate the affected date in My Timesheets (use pagination controls at the bottom if needed).
  • 2Click the row to open Timesheet Details and verify whether Check-In, Clock Out, or Work Type (WFO/WFH) is missing or inaccurate.
  • 3Take note of the exact date, expected arrival/departure time, and reason (e.g., browser GPS blocked or forgotten punch).
  • 4Report the discrepancy to your Direct Manager or HR Administrator so they can update the attendance log from the HR Admin portal.
Self-Service Note: In the current ESS portal release, Timesheet Details is read-only and there is no self-service missed-punch edit button. All attendance corrections must be applied by HR Administration.

2.3 Overtime Requests

2.3a
Overtime Submission Regular & PHL

Create & Submit an Overtime Request

Selecting an overtime date automatically pulls your recorded Clock In and Clock Out times for validation.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Overtime Form
  • 1Open Overtime Request from the sidebar to review your Approved Overtime, Pending Requests, and Direct Supervisor cards.
  • 2Click + Request Overtime at the top right to open the submission modal.
  • 3Select DATE *. If you clocked in on that date, the read-only CLOCK IN and CLOCK OUT fields populate automatically.

2.3b
Input Validations Negative Test Cases

Overtime Validation Rules & Error Alerts

SparkX blocks incomplete or policy-violating overtime entries with inline alerts before submission.

Validation Cases Covered

Error Handling
  • 1Missing Date: Submitting without selecting a date triggers "Choose the overtime date" under the Date field.
  • 2Missing Reason: Leaving the Reason box blank triggers "Say what the overtime was for".
  • 3Exceeding 16 Hours / Reversed Time: Setting From later than To (or >16h span) triggers "Overtime over 16 hours in one request is not accepted".
  • 4Break Equals or Exceeds Overtime: Entering break minutes ≥ total duration triggers "The break is as long as the overtime itself".

3.1 Leave Request (Balances, Submit, Cancel & Withdraw)

Complete guide to checking available leave balances, submitting time off requests, tracking manager approvals, and cancelling or withdrawing leave.

3.1a
Balances & History Leave Overview

Read Your Leave Balance, History & Approval Progress

View entitlements with carryover expiry dates, filter historical requests, and inspect approval stages.

Balances, Tracking, Cancel & Withdraw

Leave Overview
  • 1Read Your Balance: Click Details on the Available Leave card to see each leave type with its expiry date. Carried-over days are automatically consumed first before they expire.
  • 2Follow a Request: Click any LR-... request code (or Details) in Leave History to inspect each approval step (e.g., Line Manager Review) and see who is currently reviewing it.
  • 3Cancel a Pending Request: Open a Pending request, click Cancel This Request, and confirm with Yes, Cancel. Days return to your balance immediately and the cancelled record moves to the Audit Trail.
  • 4Withdraw Approved Leave: Open an Approved request and click Request Withdrawal (with an optional reason). Your manager must approve the withdrawal before days return to your balance.
Note on Balances & Imported Leave: Leave used in advance shows as 0 and is flagged in red (e.g. "2 days below zero"). Leave marked Imported from a previous HR system cannot be withdrawn via ESS—contact HR if an adjustment is needed.
3.1b
Submission Flow Request Time Off

Submit a Leave Request (+ Request Time Off)

Select your leave type, pick single or multi-day dates, attach mandatory documents if required, and confirm your summary.

Step-by-Step Instructions

8-Step Flow
  • 1Click + Request Time Off at the top right of the Leave Management page.
  • 2Select a Leave Type. Each eligible type shows available days; greyed-out options display the reason underneath (e.g., "Use your Sick Leave first").
  • 3Click Select dates. The calendar selects 1 day by default—toggle More than one day on to choose a start and end range, then click Confirm.
  • 4Review Calculated Working Days (which automatically excludes days off and company holidays; calendar-day leave types show Calculated Calendar Days).
  • 5If required (such as for Sick or Hospitalization leave), upload a medical certificate under Supporting Document (Mandatory), add an optional Reason, and click Review Request.
  • 6Verify the Confirm Leave Request summary and click Confirm & Submit (or Back to Edit to adjust dates).
3.1c
Policy & Guardrails Negative Test Cases

Leave Validation Rules & Blocked Submissions

SparkX enforces balance limits, overlap checks, and mandatory attachments before a request can be submitted.

Negative & Validation Cases

Guardrails
  • 1Empty Required Fields: Review Request remains disabled until valid dates (and any mandatory supporting document) are provided.
  • 2Insufficient Balance: Selecting more working days than available displays ⚠ Insufficient balance — only X days available and disables submission.
  • 3Overlapping Dates: Requests overlapping an existing Pending, Approved, or Withdrawal Pending leave are refused with the blocking LR-... code.
