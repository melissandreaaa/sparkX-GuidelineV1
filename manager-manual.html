Role-Based Manual • Manager

SparkX Manager User Manual

A structured visual reference covering every manager-specific workflow in SparkX—approvals, team leave calendars, business travel queues, expense claim reviews, reporting hierarchy, and notification settings.

Module 01: Manager Home Dashboard

Monitor pending team approvals at a glance, spot upcoming leave overlaps, and jump straight into decision workflows from your personalized Manager dashboard.

01
Dashboard /ess

Home Dashboard Overview

The Manager Home Dashboard surfaces your Pending Inbox count, team priority tasks labeled TEAM, and the Team on Leave widget.

Module 02 • Approvals

Approvals Inbox & Decision Workflows

Review individual team requests, execute bulk approvals, inspect travel/leave/PHL modals, and audit past decisions in the Archive.

02
Approvals Inbox /ess/inbox

Centralized Approvals Inbox

Under the To Do tab in Inbox, managers can search, filter by Team Approvals, and click Review on any TEAM item requiring a decision.

03
Batch Actions /ess/inbox

Bulk Selection & Batch Approval

Selecting one or more request checkboxes reveals the bottom bulk action bar, allowing you to Approve or Reject multiple team requests in a single action.

04
Travel Modal /ess/inbox

Business Travel Approval Modal

Clicking Review on a travel request opens the detail modal displaying trip dates, destination, allowance notes, and the multi-tier approval chain where you can Approve Request, Reject, or Download PDF.

05
Leave Modal /ess/inbox

Leave & Withdrawal Review Modal

Opening a leave or leave-withdrawal request from the inbox displays the employee's leave type, period, working days, and request code alongside direct Approve and Reject buttons.

06
PHL Grant Modal /ess/inbox

PHL Grant Approval Modal

For Substitute Holiday Leave (PHL) grant requests, the review modal shows the holiday date, weekend days worked, and days granted so you can Approve or Reject the entitlement.

07
Audit Trail /ess/inbox

Decision History Archive

The Archive tab maintains a complete audit trail of your past Approved and Rejected team decisions, with a View button to inspect any historical record.

Module 03 • Team Leave

Team Leave & Schedule Calendar

Manage subordinate leave queues, inspect approval timelines, and check team schedule overlaps in Week or Month views.

08
Weekly Schedule /ess/leave

Team Leave Queue & Weekly Calendar

Switching to the Team Leave tab displays your direct reports' leave approval queue above the interactive My Team Calendar in Week view for spotting schedule overlaps.

09
Details Modal /ess/leave

Leave Details & Approval Timeline

Clicking Details on a team leave row opens the full approval timeline with prior comments and lets you Approve Withdrawal or Reject pending changes.

10
Month View /ess/leave

Monthly Team Leave Calendar

Toggling My Team Calendar to Month view provides a full-month overview of approved leaves, business trips, and holidays across every direct report.

Module 04 • Team Travel

Team Business Travel

Filter subordinate business travel requests by year and status, and approve or reject trips inline.

11
Travel Queue /ess/travel

Team Travel Approval Queue

The Team Travel tab lists all subordinate business trips with year and status filters, offering inline approve/reject icons for Pending Approval rows and Details for completed trips.

Module 05 • Team Claims

Team Claim & Expenses

Filter team expense claims and review automated policy findings alongside AI Receipt Analysis.

12
Team View /ess/claim-expenses

Team Claims & Status Filters

Under Team View in Claim & Expenses, managers can filter team reimbursements by status (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Paid) and click any row to inspect it.

13
AI Receipt Review /ess/claim-expenses

Policy Findings & AI Receipt Analysis

Clicking a team claim opens the Review Request modal showing expense details, automated Policy findings, and AI Receipt Analysis comparing claimed values against the uploaded receipt.

Module 06 • Team Hierarchy

Team Reporting Hierarchy

Visualize your reporting lines, supervisor, and direct reports in an interactive organization chart.

14
Org Chart /ess/profile

Interactive Reporting Org Chart

The Organization tab in My Profile renders an interactive org chart of your supervisor, your position, and your direct reports with zoom and fit-to-view controls.

Module 07 • Settings

Manager Notifications & Account Settings

Configure approval alert preferences, organization switching, password updates, and biometric login.

15
Account Settings /ess/account

Notification & Security Controls

In Account Settings (/ess/account), managers can toggle Enable Notifications for approval alerts and reminders, switch organizations, or manage password and biometric sign-in.
