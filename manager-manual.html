SparkX Manager User Manual

Comprehensive visual guide for Managers in SparkX — covering sign-in, personalized dashboard metrics, centralized Inbox approvals (individual & bulk), Team Leave, Team Travel, Team Claims, Team Hierarchy, Document Acknowledgements, and Lifecycle tools.

Module 01: Sign-In & Manager Dashboard

Authenticate with your Manager credentials (e.g. jan.fredrick@nautilus.test), customize your dashboard KPI cards, and act on pending TEAM and ACK tasks.

01
Authentication /login

Manager Sign-In Page

Sign in to SparkX using your Manager work email and password, Google SSO, or Biometrics.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 01 of 26
  • 1Open /login in your browser and enter your Manager Email (jan.fredrick@nautilus.test) and Password.
  • 2Use the eye icon to verify your password, or click Forgot password? if you need a reset.
  • 3Click Sign in (or use Google / Sign in with Biometrics) to enter your Manager workspace.
02
Dashboard /ess

Home Dashboard & Priority Tasks

Monitor your Pending Inbox count, TEAM approval items (Travel, Leave Withdrawal, PHL Grant), ACK documents, and Team on Leave widget.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 02 of 26
  • 1Check the top Pending Inbox, Leave Balance, and Pending Claims KPI cards, or click Review Tasks > to jump straight to Inbox.
  • 2Under Priority Tasks, click Review on any TEAM item (Travel, Leave, PHL Grant) or Acknowledge on ACK company documents.
  • 3Use the Team on Leave dropdown (This Week / This Month) on the right to monitor upcoming team absences.
03
Personalization Customize Metrics

Customize Dashboard Metrics

Reorder, add, or remove up to 4 KPI cards on your Manager Home Dashboard using the Customize Metrics modal.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 03 of 26
  • 1Click + ADD METRIC on the Dashboard to open the Customize Metrics modal (max 4 items).
  • 2Use the up/down arrows to reorder active cards, or remove/add optional cards, then click Save Changes.
04
Assistant & Menu ESS Assistant

ESS AI Assistant & Sidebar

Access the floating ESS Assistant for natural-language requests, or click the gear icon next to EMPLOYEE SELF-SERVICE to customize sidebar menu order.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 04 of 26
  • 1Click the purple Hi Jan! Need help? pill in the bottom-right corner to open ESS Assistant.
  • 2Click a quick prompt chip, attach a receipt/document, or type your question in Ask the ESS assistant….
  • 3To reorder or hide sidebar items, click the gear icon beside EMPLOYEE SELF-SERVICE in the left sidebar.

Module 02: Centralized Approvals Inbox

Review individual team requests, execute bulk approvals, inspect Travel/Leave/PHL review modals, and audit past decisions in the Archive.

05
Approvals Inbox /ess/inbox

Centralized Approvals Inbox

Filter by Unread, Action Required, or Filter popover (including Team Approvals), and inspect TEAM, ACK, FYI, and NOTICE cards.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 05 of 26
  • 1Open Inbox from the left sidebar (/ess/inbox) and stay on the To Do tab.
  • 2Use Search inbox..., Unread, Action Required, or the Filter menu to focus on Team Approvals.
  • 3Click Review on any TEAM card to open its decision modal, or Mark all read to clear unread badges.
06
Batch Actions Bulk Approve / Reject

Bulk Selection & Batch Action

Select multiple TEAM items (or click Select all) to reveal the floating bottom bar with Reject (N) and Approve (N).

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 06 of 26
  • 1Click the circular checkbox on individual TEAM cards, or click Select all in the toolbar.
  • 2In the floating bottom bar, click Approve (N) or Reject (N) to process all selected items together, or click Deselect / X to cancel.
07
Travel Approval TR-MVF1XCZ7YMC

Travel Request Review Modal

Inspect trip type, departure/return dates, business reason, daily allowance notice, and multi-step Approval Progress before deciding.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 07 of 26
  • 1Click Review on a Travel item to open Travel Request Details.
  • 2Verify Trip Type, Departure / Return, Reason, and any Allowance notes (or click Download PDF for offline review).
  • 3Click Approve Request to advance to the next step (e.g. HR BU Review), or Reject with a reason.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 08 of 26

  • 2Confirm the employee name, Period, and Working Days, then click Approve or Reject.
09
Audit History Archive Tab

Decision History Archive

Review all previously approved or rejected team requests under the Archive tab's Action History list.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 09 of 26

  • 2Click View on any historical Leave or Travel entry to inspect its full decision record.

Module 10: Resignation Submission & Policy

Complete the formal resignation submission form, attach a signed exit letter, and verify the mandatory 30-day notice period policy.

25
Resignation 1. Resignation

Resignation Form & Notice Policy

Select Resignation Type, set Requested Last Working Day (enforcing the 30-day notice period), enter Reason for Leaving, and upload your signed exit letter.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Screen 25 of 26
  • 1Under 1. Resignation, verify your pre-filled Employee Name, Employee ID, and Department.
  • 2Choose Resignation Type, pick a Requested Last Working Day (≥ 30 days ahead), and fill in Reason for Leaving.
  • 3Attach your signed exit letter under Upload Completed Exit Letter, then click Save Draft or Submit Resignation.
